## 8. Analysis choices where the pre-registration is silent (conservative option)

- Wilcoxon signed-rank uses zero_method='zsplit' (zero differences stay in the ranking, split between signs), two-sided. This is more conservative than discarding zeros.
- The Holm family is every computed model x family test, Arith included, and tests at ceiling count with p = 1. Models without complete data are not tested; adding them later enlarges the Holm family, so current adjusted p-values are provisional.
- Accuracy and the paired difference use only graded calls (correct / wrong / format_fail). Items with no graded call at a level are dropped from that pair.
- Dial labels pool all three families. 'collapsed levels': |median(b) - median(a)| <= 5% of median(a), where a is the lower level. 'rejects': every call at that level is a non-quota api_error. Partial api_errors are reported but do not set the label.
- A model whose high/baseline ratio is >= 1.15 but which fails the 'honors' rule is labelled 'neither (intermediate)' rather than forced into a label.
- Cost per correct = total cost of all non-missing calls / number of correct calls. It is undefined when there are 0 correct calls.
- Quota refusals (HTTP 403 'exceeds your available quota') are missing data, never 'rejects'. Superseded runs are listed in `results/superseded.json`.
